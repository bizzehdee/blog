A tower defence game where the maths is the game. Build an equation from number and operator tiles, get it right, and the answer becomes a tower. Bigger numbers hit harder.

- Three tiers of play: Ages 10 and under. 11 to 12. 13 and up
- Enemies that need the right kind of answer: primes, odd or even, or "solving for x"
- Feedback on wrong answers without giving the answer away
- Endless waves, how far can you go?
- Fully offline. No adverts, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no data collection
