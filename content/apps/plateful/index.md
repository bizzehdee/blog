A meal planner that runs on the AI model built into your phone. Describe what you want in one sentence, then say yes or no to each meal it suggests.

- Plans 1 to 14 days of breakfasts, lunches and dinners for your household
- Instant replacements for rejected meals, and it remembers what you've turned down
- Full recipes with quantities in grams and numbered steps
- Shopping lists grouped by aisle, with quantities added up across meals
- Allergy and intolerance checks, diet settings and cooking time limits
- No internet permission, no account, no analytics, no adverts
- Requires a phone with Gemini Nano built in (recent Pixel and comparable flagships)
