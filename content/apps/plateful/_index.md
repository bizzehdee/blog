Tell it what you want in one sentence: how many days, which meals, how many people, the food you like, and how long you're willing to spend cooking. Plateful writes the plan one dish at a time. Say yes or no to each one. Anything you turn down is replaced straight away and never offered again.

- Plans breakfasts, lunches and dinners: three days free, up to fourteen with Plus
- Counts adults and children separately, so the quantities come out right
- An instant replacement for any meal you reject, and it remembers what you've turned down
- Full recipes for every meal you keep: exact quantities in grams, numbered steps, no waffle
- Shopping lists grouped by aisle, with quantities added up across meals, tickable and shareable
- Allergies and intolerances on their own list, checked against every meal and recipe before you see it
- Diets (pescatarian, vegetarian, vegan, each with a "mostly" version) and a cooking time limit
- Tell it what's already in your kitchen and it builds the meals around that
- Save plans, rename them, duplicate them, or plan a new week like an old favourite
- No account, no sign-in, no adverts
- Works on any Android phone

Plans are written by Google's AI, so the details of your request leave your phone. Plateful asks before it sends anything, the first time you plan. Your saved plans, recipes and shopping lists are kept on your phone.

**Plateful Plus** plans up to fourteen days at a time, so one shop does the week, and rewrites any recipe or whole plan you don't fancy. Monthly or yearly.

The allergy filter is a careful filter on an AI, not a medical guarantee, and the recipes are written by an AI too. Read the ingredients before you cook, and check everything is cooked through.
