Last updated: **14 October 2026**

This policy covers **The Gaffer: Football** and **The Gaffer: Rugby** (together, "the games"), published by
**Darren Horrocks**. Questions go to **dhorrocks@gmail.com**.

## The short version

The games do not collect your data. They do not send anything to the developer or to anyone else. Everything the
games store stays on your device.

## What the games store on your device

- **Saved games.** Your saved games, including the manager name you type when you start a game, the clubs, players and
  match results.
- **Settings.** Your display, text size and similar choices.
- **Logs.** Short technical logs of what the game did, to help find faults. Names of your device, your user account and
  folders are removed from them before they are written.

All of this is kept in the game's own storage on your device. You can remove it by clearing the game's data or by
uninstalling the game.

## What the games do not do

- They do not ask for an account or a sign-in.
- They do not use the internet. The Android builds ask for no permissions at all (no network, location, contacts,
  camera, microphone or storage permissions).
- They show no adverts and sell nothing.
- They contain no analytics and no crash reporting.
- They do not use your advertising ID or any other identifier.

## The diagnostic bundle

In **Diagnostics** you can choose to create a bundle: a zip file with the game's log, technical details of your device
(for example the screen size and the amount of memory) and a copy of your latest save. The game creates the file only
when you ask for it and saves it where you choose. It is not sent anywhere. If you give it to the developer to report a
problem, you are the one sharing it. A save in the bundle contains the manager name you typed, so do not use your real
name in the game if you do not want it shared that way.

## Store and platform

The store you download the games from (for example Google Play) collects its own information about your download and
device under its own privacy policy. The games have no access to it.

## Children

The games contain no chat, no user-generated content and no way to contact other players, and they collect no personal
information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a later version collects any data, for example opt-in crash reports, this policy will be updated and the
change described **before** that version is released, and nothing will be sent unless you turn it on.

## Your rights

The developer holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete. Your saved games and
settings are on your device under your control.
