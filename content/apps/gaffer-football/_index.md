The Gaffer: Football is a single-player football management game. You manage a men's or a women's team in a world of clubs, players and competitions that is generated for each new game, so every career is different. All names are fictional.

- Pick the squad and the team for every match, and name a captain
- Set the tactics from clear settings. How well a tactic works depends on the players you have
- Run the weekly training schedule and see how players grow over the seasons
- Buy, sell and loan players. Negotiate contracts with wages, bonuses and release clauses
- Keep the club's finances in order with a budget, income and expenses
- Send scouts to find players. Reports show some of what a player can do and hide the rest
- Play leagues over many seasons with promotion and relegation. Men's and women's teams play alongside each other
- Watch matches as a live top-down view with an event feed and statistics, or skip to the result
- Make substitutions during a match
- Choose one of four colour themes, including a colour-blind theme and a high contrast theme
- Move a save to another device with an exported file
