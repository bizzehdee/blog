Many phones save photos at a much higher quality than you can see. Reclaim re-encodes them at a quality chosen to look the same, and keeps everything else about each photo: the date it was taken, its location if it has one, the camera details, which way up it goes, and the album it is in.

- Scan your library and see how much space each photo and video could save, before anything is changed
- Preview a sample of your own photos before and after, side by side, with the sizes
- Every new file is checked before it replaces the original. Anything that fails a check is left exactly as it was
- Long runs keep going in the background and pick up where they left off if interrupted
- RAW files, Motion Photos, PNG screenshots and already-compressed photos are skipped, and Reclaim says why
- Optionally remove location and other details from photos as they are optimized
- Includes photos and videos on an SD card
- Everything happens on your phone. Nothing is uploaded
- No account, no sign-in, no adverts
- Needs Android 11 or later

Free users can optimize up to 500 photos in total. **Pro**, a one-time purchase with no subscription, removes the limit, optimizes videos too (re-encoded to HEVC where your phone's hardware can do it), and lets you set the compression level yourself.

Optimizing is permanent. The original is replaced and there is no undo. If your photos are backed up to a cloud service, Reclaim frees space on your phone, not in the cloud.

Usage and crash reports are sent only if you agree, and never include anything about your photos.
